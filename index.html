About the tracker

How to use

  1. Add teams you follow in Favorite teams to track their results.
  2. In Add a game, enter the sport, teams, date, and venue. Score and notes are optional.
  3. Select Won beside the winner. Favorite-team results update the W/L markers and overall record.
  4. Pick a venue from the list (or type a home team and it fills in) and the game gets a pin on Your game map.

Games and favorites are saved in this browser and are not sent to a server. The map loads tiles from OpenStreetMap, and a venue we don't know is sent to OpenStreetMap to look up its location.
